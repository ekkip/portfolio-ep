CO2 AR
Mobile AR experience visualising the history and projected future of global CO2 emissions
Role
Interaction design
ARKit coding

Cooperation
Julian Braun

Client
Urban Complexity Lab

Background
In 2017, the Urban Complexity Lab at FH Potsam partnered with Potsdam Institute for Climate Impact Research to produce an information film about the history of carbon dioxide emissions and their cumulative nature, from the Industrial Revolution until now, and further on into the year 2100 with two differing future scenarios.
Details

The iOS app CO2 AR was developed as a spin-off. The use of AR was an attempt to strike out the urgency of the matter. The central info-vis element of the film, a globe with a 3-D cumulation chart, is placed in AR space and can be rotated. The cumulation can be controlled with a time slider and a switch for the two future scenarios.
